Ke Huang, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Mathematics and Statistics

University of Massachusetts Lowell

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Massachusetts Lowell. Previously, I was a Research Associate in the Department of Radiology at Weill Cornell Medicine & Cornell University working with Dr. Amy Kuceyeski (CoCo Lab). I earned my Ph.D. in Applied Statistics from the University of California, Riverside in 2024 under the supervision of Dr. Shujie Ma.

My research focuses on:

  • (1) Statistical & AI methodologies for neuroscience and biomedical data—including human brain connectomes, women's brain health, EEG, and ECG signal analysis.
  • (2) Statistical learning theory for deep neural networks.

Research Interests

Statistical & AI Applications in Health

Developing artificial intelligence and statistical models to analyze complex biomedical and health data—such as human brain connectivity (connectomes), Electroencephalogram (EEG), and Electrocardiogram (ECG)—providing insights and methodological advancements.

Brain Connectomes Women's Brain Health EEG / ECG Signal Analysis Biostatistics

Statistical Learning Theory of Neural Networks

Providing theoretical foundations for deep learning, including approximation theory and statistical estimation theory of neural networks, and exploring methodologies to overcome high-dimensional challenges.

Deep Learning Theory Sparse Neural Networks Empirical Risk Minimization High-Dimensional Statistics

I am actively looking for motivated students to join my research group at UMass Lowell who are interested in neuroscience and women's brain health.

  • Neuroscience & Women's Brain Health
  • Statistics / Mathematics / Deep Learning Background
  • Strong Coding Skills (Python / R)

Prospective students with a solid background in statistics, mathematics, or deep learning along with strong programming skills are encouraged to reach out at Ke_Huang@uml.edu with your CV, transcripts, and a brief description of your research interests.
